Wal-Star, Inc. E450B413DoveLegRestFootRestCovers

by Wal-Star, INC.

What this type of device does: A manually-operated device designed to support the patient in a seated or reclined posture during haemodialysis procedures. It is usually made of easily washable materials, with appropriate arm, back, and footrests that can be adjusted by the patient manually. It provides interface with the dialysis system, facilitating the patient connection to the extracorporeal blood circuit.
