MRI Patient Monitor TeslaDUO

by Mipm Mammendorfer Institut Für Physik Und Medizin GmbH

The MRI Patient Monitor TeslaDUO is intended for monitoring of vital signs during MRI examinations (MRI procedures) of patients.

What this type of device does: A mobile assembly of devices designed for continuous assessment of several vital physiologic parameters (e.g., ECG, blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, cardiac output, apnoea, and respiratory/anaesthetic gas concentrations) of infant to adult intensive care patients during a magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) procedure. It includes an MRI patient monitor, sensors/electrodes (e.g., ECG electrodes, pulse oximetry telemetric transmitter, blood pressure cuff), and typically an additional remote monitor.
