Anti-panHb APC IQP-575A

by Iq Products B.v.

The APC conjugated monoclonal antibody anti-pan Hemoglobin (panHb) antibody recognizes the α-chain of human hemoglobin.

What this type of device does: One or multiple immunoglobulins capable of binding to specific antigenic determinants and intended to be used for the qualitative and/or quantitative detection of haemoglobin in a clinical specimen.
