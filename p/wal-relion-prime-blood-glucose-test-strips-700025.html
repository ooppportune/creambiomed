ReliOn® Prime Blood Glucose Test Strips 700025

by Wal-Mart Stores, INC.

Blood Glucose Test Strips

What this type of device does: A collection of devices including a portable, battery-powered, semi-automated or automated instrument (self-testing meter), reagents, test strips and/or other associated materials and accessories (e.g., control solutions, lancets) intended to be used for the quantitative measurement of glucose in a whole blood clinical specimen; it is designed to be used for self-testing by a layperson in the home, and some types may in addition be used at the point-of-care. Measured glucose values are used to manage blood glucose levels, primarily by persons with diabetes mellitus.
