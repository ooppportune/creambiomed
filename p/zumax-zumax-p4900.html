zumax P4900

by Zumax Medical CO., LTD

Ophthalmoscope Bulb,3.5V

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered, hand-held, ophthalmic instrument designed to be held close to the patient’s eye to examine the interior of the eye and related structures [e.g., fundus (retina), cornea, aqueous, lens, and vitreous] by producing an upright (unreversed) image of approximately 15 times magnification. It consists of a main unit (commonly referred to as the head) with a built-in light source directed through the pupil to illuminate the interior of the eyeball, a mirror with a single hole for viewing, and a dial containing lenses of different powers; a dedicated power supply handpiece may be included with the head.
