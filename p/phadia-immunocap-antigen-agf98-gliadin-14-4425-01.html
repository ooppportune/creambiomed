ImmunoCAP Antigen AGf98, Gliadin

What this type of device does: A collection of reagents and other associated materials intended to be used for the qualitative and/or quantitative detection of anti-gliadin antibodies in a clinical specimen, using a fluorescent immunoassay method. This test is intended to be used as an aid in the diagnosis of Coeliac disease.
