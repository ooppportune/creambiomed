Rescuer MVP Manual Vacuum Pump 7000-MVP

by B.l.s. Systems Limited

Rescuer MVP Manual Vacuum Pump with Collection jar and two oral suction catheters

What this type of device does: A manually-powered, portable device, which may include noninvasive accessories, intended to generate negative pressure for emergency aspiration of fluids, secretions, or other unwanted materials from the airway of a patient, in conjunction with a handpiece/tip (not included). It is typically used during patient transport or for emergency situations (e.g., placed on a crash cart); it is not intended for surgical use. The pump may include a collection container, microbial filter, valve, or tubing. This is a reusable device.
