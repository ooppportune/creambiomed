PVS 1625, 115 V, Viracor USA 90.2063.049

by Sarstedt Aktiengesellschaft & CO.kg

What this type of device does: An assembly of electrically-powered robotic devices, a stand-alone device, or a belt conveyor intended to be used for the automated or semi-automated transfer of assay containers (e.g., test tubes, microplates), microscope slides/slide carriers, or racks between processing stations. The system may interface with individual instruments/analysers and other equipment, transporting assay containers between stations which may include specimen pipetting, reagent dispensing, incubation, microplate washing, agitation, barcode reading and/or optical reading.
