Anprolene AN75 Refill Kit, 14 Units AN7514.00

by Andersen Sterilizers INC.

Anprolene AN75 Refill Kit, 14 Units - 14 EO 17.6g cartridges, 14 AN87 Dosimeters, 15 AN75 liner bags

What this type of device does: A mains electricity (AC-powered) device designed for total elimination and/or inactivation of microorganisms from medical devices and related products using ethylene oxide (ETO) gas with low humidity content as the sterilizing agent; it is typically used for products sensitive to high temperature and humidity. It typically includes a treatment chamber with shelves where the devices to be sterilized are placed, usually after cleaning of gross debris and packed in gas permeable wraps; a means to introduce the gas into the chamber; and controls to chose one of the sterilization cycles. The device is available in a variety of shapes and sizes, including stand-alone (bulk) and tabletop units.
