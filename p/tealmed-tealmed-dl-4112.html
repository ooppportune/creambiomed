Tealmed DL-4112

by Ningbo Tealmed Emergency Products CO., LTD.

What this type of device does: A hand-held electric component of a direct otoscope and/or ophthalmoscope intended to facilitate manipulation of, and supply electrical current to, the otoscope/ophthalmoscope head (not included). It typically includes a rechargeable battery (either incorporated or removable) with a power switch, and may include a battery charger. This is a reusable device.
