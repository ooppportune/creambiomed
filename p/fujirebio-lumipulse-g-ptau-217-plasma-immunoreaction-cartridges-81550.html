by Fujirebio Europe

Lumipulse G pTau 217 Plasma Immunoreaction Cartridges 3x14T, IVD-US

What this type of device does: A collection of reagents and other associated materials intended to be used for the qualitative and/or quantitative detection of microtubule-associated tau protein [total tau (t-tau) or phosphorylated tau (p-tau)] in a clinical specimen, using a chemiluminescent immunoassay method. It is typically used to aid the diagnosis of neurodegenerative disorders such as Alzheimer's disease.
