Fisherbrand 10-356

by Fisher Scientific Company L.l.c.

DOUBLE CYTO FUNNEL WCARD 25PK

What this type of device does: A small, tapered receptacle designed to be fitted into a cytological centrifuge/sample concentrator, and intended to contain and help concentrate clinical specimen suspension cells into thin-layer preparations by depositing them directly onto a microscope slide during centrifugation for subsequent examination. It is in the form of a conical-shaped plastic receptacle; some types have a slot for inserting a microscope slide. This is a single-use device.
