LifeBand® Load-distributing Band 100

by Zoll Circulation, INC.

Single-use chest compression band. (3 per package)

What this type of device does: A wide belt intended to be applied across the chest as part of an automated cardiac resuscitator, for the application of rhythmic compression to the chest in the region of the heart to provide blood flow during treatment of cardiac arrest [cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR)]. The device is typically used on adult patients only and may be adjusted to accommodate a range of chest circumferences. This is a single-use device.
