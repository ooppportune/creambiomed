ASSY, REPLACEMENT, HANA BASE, 100V 6875-326j

by Mizuho Orthopedic Systems, INC.

6875-326j = 6875-326J

What this type of device does: A mobile, electrically-powered motorized table designed to support a patient and to provide traction for the limb(s)/spine of the patient during orthopaedic surgical procedures. Its surface consists of articulated sections that can be adjusted, and it is often intended to be used with additional patient positioners. It may have x-ray transparent features.
