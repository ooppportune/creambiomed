BD MiniDraw™ Cap Removal Tool 366618

by Becton, Dickinson And Company

BD MiniDraw™ Cap Removal Tool

What this type of device does: A small, manually-operated device intended to be used to remove the cap/lid from a capillary blood collection tube, prior to subsequent in vitro diagnostic analysis of the clinical specimen. It clamps onto the cap/lid and removes it from the tube with a twisting/lifting motion. It is typically made from synthetic polymer materials (e.g., plastic). This is a reusable device.
