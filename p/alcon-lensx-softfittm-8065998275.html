LenSx® SoftFit™ 8065998275

by Alcon Laboratories, INC.

LenSx® SoftFit™ Patient Interface Steep

What this type of device does: A non-powered device intended to orient and fix the eyeball in a desired position relative to an ophthalmic laser system beam guide during ophthalmic laser surgery. It is designed as a laser-interfacing suction cup intended to contact and stabilize the eyeball via suction controlled by the laser system; it may also include a treatment contact lens. This is a single-use device.
