Leica CV5030-TS5015 149CVTS5015

by Leica Biosystems Nussloch GmbH

The Leica CV5030 is a robotic coverslipper for coverslipping tissue sections, cells or swabs mounted on specimen slides with cover slips made out of glass using a wide variety of differ¬ent cover slip mountants. It may only be used with instrument accessories approved by Leica Biosystems Nussloch GmbH.

What this type of device does: A thin piece of glass or a plastic sheath placed over a specimen on a microscope slide, to serve as a physical barrier to protect the specimen from mechanical forces and environmental exposure. This is a single-use device.
