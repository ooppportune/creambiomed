HARTON NBM-6B

by Dongguan Simzo Electronic Technology CO., LTD.

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered, hand-held, portable device intended to be used outside of a clinical setting (e.g., home, outdoors) to aerosolize medication/fluids, by non-heat-dependent means (i.e., not a vaporizer), for inhalation by a patient, typically to treat/manage a respiratory disorder [e.g., asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), cystic fibrosis (CF)]. It may employ various technologies (ultrasonic, vibrating mesh, or compressor jet type) and consists of a pocket-size unit containing a reservoir to be filled with medication/fluids, and a retractable mouthpiece or attached mask. This device is not a nebulizing chamber.
