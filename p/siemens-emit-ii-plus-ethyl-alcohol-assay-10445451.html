Emit® II Plus Ethyl Alcohol Assay 10445451

by Siemens Healthcare Diagnostics INC.

Emit ® II Plus Ethyl Alcohol Assay labeled with the Beckman Coulter® name

What this type of device does: A collection of reagents and other associated materials intended to be used for the qualitative and/or quantitative detection of ethyl alcohol (ETOH) in a clinical specimen, using a spectrophotometry method. It is typically used to assess for alcohol intoxication and in the management of alcoholism.
