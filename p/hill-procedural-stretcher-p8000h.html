Procedural stretcher P8000H

by Hill-Rom De México, S. De R.l. De C.v.

Hospital Stretcher

What this type of device does: A manually-operated device consisting of a platform mounted on a wheeled frame designed primarily to facilitate easy transport of a recumbent patient within a healthcare facility; it may include side rails, supports for fluid infusion equipment, and patient securement straps. The device may also be used as a treatment table (e.g., in an emergency, recovery, or labour room), or as a bed in some intensive and specialized care units or under high patient census conditions (e.g., disasters).
