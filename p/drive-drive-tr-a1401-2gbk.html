Product Description Trotter Headrest Extension, 14", Black

What this type of device does: A padded device attached to a wheelchair, a disability-supportive chair, and/or a stander to provide a disabled patient with additional head support and comfort while in a sitting and/or standing position. This device will also include the extensions (e.g., a metal insert, screws) needed to attach this device. It may be available in various shapes and sizes, and it may be adjustable.
