Dialyzer Dialysate QD to 45° Angle Hose Barb Red D300-0105

by Reprocessing Products Corporation

What this type of device does: An assembly of devices intended to supply large-scale haemodialysis dialysate (concentrate and/or ready-to-use solution) to medical facility haemodialysis systems for preparation/mixing and/or direct use. The system is typically built into the facility and consists of a pump(s) which applies positive pressure at a pre-set rate to supply the solution, storage tank(s), connection pipe(s), pressure and/or quantity sensor(s), and a control panel.
