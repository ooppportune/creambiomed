Zephyr Patient Positioning and Transfer System ZXL-ASB-01

by Diacor, INC.

Zephyr Docking Side Stop Bar - (1): Used on 53cm Varian Couch

What this type of device does: A lifting device used together with a patient lifting system to provide a support for an incapacitated patient or a person with a disability that is being lifted in a lying (horizontal) position for transfer and (re)positioning. It is designed as an elongated framework with a solid or soft surface upon which the person to be lifted is placed and contained during the lifting process.
