CRYOcheck Chromogenic Factor IX CCCF09

by Precision Biologic INC

CRYOcheck Chromogenic Factor IX is for clinical laboratory use in the quantitative determination of factor IX activity in 3.2% citrated human plasma. It is intended to be used in identifying factor IX deficiency and as an aid in the management of hemophilia B in individuals aged 2 years and older. For in vitro diagnostic use

What this type of device does: A collection of reagents and other associated materials intended to be used for the quantitative detection of coagulation factor IX in a clinical specimen, using a chromogenic method. Factor IX deficiency may result in a hereditary bleeding disorder known as haemophilia B or Christmas disease.
