Powerheart® AED G3 Plus Semi Automatic 9390E-1001

by Zoll Medical Corporation

G3Plus, AED, Semi Automatic, AHA 2010, with adult pads and battery

What this type of device does: A portable electronic device designed to automatically detect cardiac arrhythmias (ventricular fibrillation/pulseless ventricular tachycardia) in a sudden cardiac arrest (SCA) patient, after which it audibly/visually instructs an operator to enable it to activate defibrillation of the heart through application of electrical shocks to the chest surface. It is placed in conspicuous public locations and is intended to be operated by a layperson. It consists of two skin-adhesive, cartridge-type, disposable electrodes containing non-rechargeable batteries that provide the shock energy, and an electrode-regulating external pulse generator (EPG), which may include a CPR feedback.
