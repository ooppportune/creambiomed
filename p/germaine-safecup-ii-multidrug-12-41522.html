SAFECUP II MULTIDRUG 12 41522

by Germaine Laboratories, INC

Lateral flow immunoassay for the detection of drugs of abuse in urine. Flat-side cup for easy photocopying. Tests for COC, AMP, mAMP, THC, MTD, MDMA, OXY, OPI, PCP, BAR, BZO, TCA

What this type of device does: A collection of reagents and other associated materials intended to be used for the qualitative and/or quantitative detection of propoxyphene in a clinical specimen, using an enzyme immunoassay (EIA) method.
