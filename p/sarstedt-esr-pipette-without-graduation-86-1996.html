ESR pipette without graduation 86.1996

by Sarstedt Aktiengesellschaft & CO.kg

What this type of device does: An open-ended glass or plastic tube, or other dedicated vessel, intended to be used alone or in combination with other IVDs (e.g., ESR analyser, scaled ruler) for the quantitative determination of the erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR) of a whole blood clinical specimen. This is a single-use device.
