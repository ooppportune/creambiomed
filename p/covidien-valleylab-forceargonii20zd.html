Valleylab FORCEARGONII20ZD

by Covidien Lp

Argon Gas Delivery Unit II 120 V

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered component of an inert gas-enhanced electrosurgical system that serves as the source of inert gas [e.g., argon (Ar), helium (He)] and generates a high frequency electrical current in selected radio-frequency (RF) bands between two electrodes to produce heat in tissues for cutting and coagulating during surgery. The gas is delivered via this device and through a connected handpiece unit that ensures that the gas is channelled around the cutting electrode; a process which lessens the charring of tissue and provides rapid, uniform coagulation over large bleeding surfaces.

Valleylab™ FT10 Electrosurgical Generator

The Valleylab™ FT10 electrosurgical generator drives monopolar and bipolar devices, deploying TissueFect™ sensing technology to adjust energy output.
