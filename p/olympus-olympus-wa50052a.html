Olympus WA50052A

by Olympus Winter & Ibe GmbH

Video telescope "ENDOEYE HD II", 5.4 mm, 30°, autoclavable

What this type of device does: An endoscope with a rigid inserted portion intended for the visual examination and treatment of the organs/structures of the abdominal/retroperitoneal cavity. It is inserted through a dedicated incision made in the abdominal wall during laparoscopy. Images are transmitted by a camera/video system to a monitor for viewing by the user. This is a reusable device.
