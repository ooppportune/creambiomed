PlaDuo System

by Shenb CO., LTD.

What this type of device does: A mobile assembly of devices intended to treat a variety of skin conditions (e.g., acne scars, actinic keratosis, wrinkles, superficial skin lesions, warts) through controlled heating of the skin surface via application of nitrogen plasma. It consists of a mains electricity (AC-powered) generator/control unit and a hand-held applicator; it may include a N2 cylinder(s) and additional hardware (e.g., foot-switch). Typically radio-frequency energy is used to convert N2 into N2 plasma which exits the handpiece as a pulsed beam. This device is intended to be used by a professional in a clinical setting.
