Crystaline II 5101C-II-CE

by Lcr Hallcrest, LLC.

Crystaline II Liquid Crystal Temperature Trend Indicator - Dispenser of 100 - CE Marked

What this type of device does: A device designed to measure a patient's skin, oral, rectal, or axillary (armpit) body temperature. It uses liquid-crystal sensors (usually formed from esters of cholesterol) that are sealed in a plastic band and appear to change colour at indicated temperatures. The device may be used in the home. This is a single-use device.
