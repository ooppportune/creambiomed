E2 Easy-Set Fixed Mounting Hardware ESFX

by Motion Concepts INC

E2 Easy-Set Fixed Mounting Hardware (pair). Optional mounting hardware for Matrx E2 Backs (18H/20H). Requires tools for removal.

What this type of device does: A bracket-like device intended to be attached to the back canes of a wheelchair to accept the wheelchair back rest (not included) and allow for the adjustment of the back rest position (e.g., angle, depth, height). It is typically in the form of a clamp with hooks of adjustable position/orientation; pins intended to secure positioning may also be included. This is a reusable device.
