CARDINAL HEALTH CAS20078

by Cardinal Health 200, LLC

What this type of device does: A platform (typically metal with or without a padded vinyl covering) usually mounted on a wheelchair in pairs, and upon which a wheelchair occupant may place the feet/legs for comfort, support, and/or to keep them off of the ground. It typically includes a mounting frame, footrest, and may include a mounted pad(s) to support the calves of the legs; it may be fixed or adjustable.
