DCI 5423

by Dci International, LLC

Cabinet Mounted Assist. Instrumentation Premium 4 Position Precision Comfort, Assist.'s Pkg.

What this type of device does: A pneumatically-powered device designed to make available the necessary instruments/equipment and power to deliver dental treatment to a patient. It consists of a platform for the display of a selection of powered dental instruments (e.g., motor handpiece, scaler, suction, air/water spray), sometimes with a control panel for setting various operating parameters; it may be portable or mounted. The device is commonly referred to as a dental unit or dental operative unit and is intended to be operated by a healthcare professional in a medical facility.
