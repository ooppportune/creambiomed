AcuityPDR Standard

by Radmedix, LLC

What this type of device does: An assembly of devices that comprise a general-purpose portable diagnostic x-ray system used in a variety of routine planar x-ray imaging applications. It uses digital techniques for image capture, display and manipulation. The portable design allows it to operate on either mains or battery power and to be easily disassembled, moved from location to another, and easily reassembled for use. It consists of modular configurations that can be upgraded by the addition of hardware/software components. This GMDN code does not cover systems with fluoroscopic or tomographic capabilities.
