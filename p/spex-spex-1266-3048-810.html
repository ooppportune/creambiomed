Spex 1266-3048-810

by Spex Limited

Axial Biangular Lateral Pad Size 8, with 24mm Pressure Reduction padding, Comfy-cover

What this type of device does: A prefabricated (non-customized) padded and contoured device designed to attach to the back/seat of a wheelchair to provide the wheelchair occupant with head and trunk postural support and comfort. It may include armrests, a cushioned seat, a belt/harness, thigh support, and/or a tray table. It might additionally be designed for use as a car seat to provide support in vehicles during transportation. This is a reusable device.
