Ninjo FG2B032HEUS1

by Arjohuntleigh Ab

Ninjo flusher disinfector, FD1615

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered unit designed to both wash (remove organic debris, dirt, stains) and thermally disinfect (destroy microorganisms) reusable devices such as bedpans, urine bottles, suction bottles, emesis basins (kidney bowls), handwash basins and similar reusable devices. The device includes an internal chamber containing baskets/racks onto which the medical devices are placed to help maximize contact with all surfaces and parts. It is used in hospitals, long-term care facilities, nursing homes and healthcare centres.
