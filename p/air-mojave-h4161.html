Mojave H4161

by Air Techniques, INC.

The MOJAVE creates vacuum that will be used in a dental facility. This vacuum system is designed for reliable operation in the modern dental facility. The system uses a 100% oil-less vacuum pump to produce the high-volume air flow required for multiple simultaneous users while the separator tank ensures that no liquids enter the pump. In addition, MOJAVE incorporates an efficient energy management system. This is accomplished by adding a Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) to the Vacuum Pump, which is controlled by a Master Controller. This controller automatically adjusts the frequency of the pump to maintain the required vacuum level depending on the needs of your dental facility. With this balanced system, each user always has the flow rate necessary to do the job while conserving electricity and prolonging the life of the pump.

What this type of device does: An assembly of devices designed to aspirate water, blood, saliva, and tooth debris from the oral cavity by means of suction, to maintain a clear operative site during dental surgical, professional hygiene, and orthodontic procedures. It consists of an electrically-powered suction pump with a control knob and patient contact devices (typically suction handpiece/tip); collection containers, spittoon, tubing, and amalgam separator are also often included.
