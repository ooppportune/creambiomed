Norav 1200 Series PC-ECG BlueECG

by Norav Medical LTD.

PC-ECG Wireless Acquisition Device for Resting-ECG with IEC type labelling

What this type of device does: A body-worn, battery-powered component of an electrocardiography telemetric monitoring system designed to continuously measure and wirelessly transmit real-time electrocardiographic (ECG) signals [and sometimes additional parameters such as heart rate, skin temperature, or haemoglobin oxygen saturation (SpO2)] to a remote receiver (e.g., central station server); it may in addition record/analyse data during a period of time for later retrieval (Holter reporting). The transmitter typically includes an antenna and attached sensors (e.g., leads and electrodes) that are connected to the patient, and may be provided with dedicated data management/communication software. This is a reusable device.
