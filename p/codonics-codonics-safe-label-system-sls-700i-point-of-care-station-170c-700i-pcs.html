Codonics Safe Label System®(SLS) 700i Point of Care Station - 170C 700i PCS

by Codonics INC.

RFID Series Safe Label System automated barcode scan and labeling 170C, Feature Keyed OEM, Double Stack HF Print and Read

What this type of device does: A stand-alone assembly of devices on wheels for mobility designed to continuously or intermittently administer a mixture of gases [e.g., oxygen (O2), ambient/medical air, nitrous oxide (N2O), anaesthetic agents] to provide a patient with an appropriate level of anaesthesia for a surgical procedure. Also known as an anaesthesia machine, it may include an oxygen concentrator or function with mounted medical gas cylinders, and typically has mechanical gas-delivery devices (e.g., flowmeters and vaporizers). It may incorporate a powered or manual anaesthesia ventilator, a patient breathing circuit, and gas monitoring devices; patient monitors are typically separately mounted (not integral).
