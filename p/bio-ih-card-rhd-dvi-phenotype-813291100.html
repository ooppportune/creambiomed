IH-Card RhD(DVI-)+Phenotype 813291100

by Bio-Rad Medical Diagnostics GmbH

Blood Grouping Reagent; Gel card for use with the IH-system; Card for Rhesus phenotyping; 48 cards of 6 microtubes

What this type of device does: A collection of immunoglobulins capable of binding to specific antigenic determinants and intended to be used together in testing a clinical specimen to determine the Rh phenotype (CcDEe) [RH:1,2,3,4,5] and Kell phenotype [KEL:1,2] of red blood cells, using an agglutination method.
