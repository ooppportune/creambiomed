Triage 88753

by Quidel Cardiovascular INC.

Quidel Triage Total 5 Control 1

What this type of device does: A material which is used to verify the performance of an assay intended to be used for the qualitative and/or quantitative detection of one or a combination of cardiac markers in a clinical specimen. The cardiac markers may include B-type natriuretic protein, D-dimer, creatine kinase myocardial isoenzyme (CKMB), myoglobin, myeloperoxidase (MPO), fatty acid binding protein (FABP), glycogen phosphorylase isoenzyme BB (GPBB), troponin I and/or troponin T.
