Radical Docking Station RDS-1 signal extraction pulse oximeter 9021

by Masimo Corporation

The device has the Direct Mark DI of: 00843997013802

What this type of device does: A mains electricity (AC-powered) photoelectric device intended for the continuous transcutaneous measurement and display of haemoglobin oxygen saturation (SpO2). The signals, typically produced by light-emitting diodes (LEDs) and a receiving detector in a probe, or directly built-in, are used to make the measurements using the principle of spectrophotometry. The oximeter displays the SpO2 values and may calculate/display other parameters, e.g., pulse rate, electrocardiogram (ECG). The device is typically used bedside.
