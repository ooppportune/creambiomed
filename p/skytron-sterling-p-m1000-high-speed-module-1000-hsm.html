Sterling P/M1000 High speed module 1000-HSM

by Skytron LLC

High speed module, 208V

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered unit intended to both wash and disinfect a range of surgical instruments, and typically also ancillary utensils (e.g., containers), using both heat and a disinfectant solution; it is not dedicated to washing/disinfecting a specific device type. The device includes an internal chamber containing baskets/racks onto which the devices are placed to help maximize contact with all surfaces and parts. It is used in hospitals typically in a reprocessing suite.
