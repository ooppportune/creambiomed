C-Thru C-Thru Adult Armboard Pad

5" thick pad 11" x 11" - C-Thru Adult Armboard

What this type of device does: A component of a modular operating table designed as a detachable table top to support a patient during orthopaedic surgical procedures. Its surface consists of articulated sections that can be adjusted; it might be intended to provide traction, and may have x-ray transparent features. It is often intended to be used with additional patient positioners.
