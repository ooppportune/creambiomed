Vacuette 456089

by Greiner Bio-One GmbH

VACUETTE® TUBE 6 ml Z Serum Clot Activator 13x100 red cap-black ring, non-ridged

What this type of device does: A glass or plastic tube, sealed with a stopper, containing a pre-measured amount of vacuum and a clot activator. It is intended to be used for the collection, and preservation and/or transport, of blood for analysis and/or other investigation (e.g., serum chemistry determinations). This is a single-use device.
