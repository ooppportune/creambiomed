What this type of device does: A manually-operated device consisting of a platform mounted on a wheeled frame designed for use by emergency medical services (EMS) primarily to facilitate easy transport of a recumbent patient to and from ambulance vehicles (e.g., automobiles, aeroplanes, helicopters, boats); it does not include a powered or powered-assisted lifting mechanism. It is typically constructed of lightweight materials and has an undercarriage that opens and folds when it is removed from or pushed into the ambulance; it also usually includes locking devices that match with the locking/docking devices in the ambulance.
