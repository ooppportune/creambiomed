PLUS P7203-03-010

by Lifeport, LLC

PLUS, 72 IN HIGH G STRUCTURE

What this type of device does: A device designed to be installed within an ambulance to facilitate the manual docking and locking of an ambulance stretcher to an ambulance, and secure the stretcher during transportation. It consists of a docking point(s) that may include a manual lifting/guiding mechanism.
