Olympus WA90004W

by Olympus Winter & Ibe GmbH

Argon Plasma Coagulation unit APU-300

What this type of device does: A device designed to deliver argon (Ar) gas from an Ar gas source to an argon-enhanced electrosurgical system. It is typically incorporated into a trolley (cart) and is connected to the electrosurgical diathermy generator, enabling a single control for the system. The argon gas is delivered through an appropriate handpiece and active electrode (not included) to minimize tissue charring and enhance coagulation at and around the site of operation.
