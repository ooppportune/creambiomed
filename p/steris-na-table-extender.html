NA Table Extender

by Steris Corporation

The Bariatric Extender 4-inch Pads for the Foot Section X-ray Top and can be used with the 3080 and or 3085 SP General Surgical Table.

What this type of device does: A component of a modular operating table designed as a detachable table top that can be adjusted to support a patient during many types of surgical interventions. It may consists of many articulated sections that can be elevated or lowered for contouring to accommodate numerous anatomical positions (e.g., the whole table top may be adjusted to form a curved surface), or intended to be used in conjunction with dedicated accessories (i.e., positioners, traction devices) to satisfy the requirements of many clinical specialties; it may have x-ray transparent features.

Bariatric Width Extenders without Pads | STERIS

Bariatric table pad extenders provide an extra 8" (4" on each side) of surgical table width on all four sections of the table. Easily attach onto the siderail of the surgical table by sliding into place
