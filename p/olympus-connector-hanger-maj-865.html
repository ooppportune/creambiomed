CONNECTOR HANGER MAJ-865

by Olympus Medical Systems Corp.

What this type of device does: A vertical mount/pole onto which an endoscope connector is designed to be mounted to facilitate loading and holding an endoscope in an endoscope washer/disinfector for a washing/disinfection cycle. It is typically affixed to the side of the washer/disinfector whereby the upper end of the hanger remains above the instrument. This is a reusable device.
