Otoscope Assembly iPhone XR

by Tympa Health Technologies Limited

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered portable device intended for the visual examination of the outer ear canal/tympanic membrane by video otology, and basic evaluation of hearing function. It consists of a speculum with integrated video camera, light source, pitch generator (speaker), and a dedicated smartphone for image display and data relay to a central datastore; it may provide a conduit for a microsuction device.
