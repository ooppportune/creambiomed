MR Patient Care Universal Gating Interface

by Philips Ds North America LLC

What this type of device does: A physiological monitoring unit used as a component of a diagnostic magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) system, that produces a signal which enables image formation or data collection to be synchronized with a specific measurable physiological parameter, e.g., the beginning of a patient's respiratory or cardiac cycle. It is primarily used for the purpose of artifact reduction or enhancement of signal-to-noise ratios in various real-time or dynamic MRI applications. It is sometimes referred to as a gated imaging accessory or trigger.
