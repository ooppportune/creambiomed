SOL-CARE™ Safety Blood Collection Needle 23G*3/4*12inch (US)

What this type of device does: A cap/cover intended to be extended over a used needle (attached to a syringe/autoinjector or not) to prevent accidental needle sticks, typically during removal of the needle. It is typically a plastic housing with appropriate shielding to prevent injury upon application, and is typically disposed of with the needle; it is not the needle cap provided with the needle. This is a single-use device.
