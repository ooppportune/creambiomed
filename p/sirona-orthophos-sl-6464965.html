ORTHOPHOS SL 6464965

by Sirona Dental Systems GmbH

OP SL 3D 8x8

What this type of device does: A diagnostic digital dental x-ray system designed for permanent fixture in one location with an extraoral x-ray sensor and source intended to generate and control x-ray beams used in advanced dental imaging applications involving the teeth, jaw, oral cavity, sinus, and/or other maxillofacial structures. It uses digital techniques for image capture, manipulation and display and may include various image processing and analysis capabilities. This system is designed with the capability to produce two or more special purpose dental x-ray examinations: linear scanning, panoramic, cephalometric, linear tomography, spiral tomography, scanograms.
