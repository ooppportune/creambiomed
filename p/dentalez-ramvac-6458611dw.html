Ramvac 6458611DW

by Dentalez Alabama, INC.

MC-201 PUMP W/RECIRC 230V 60HZ

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered device intended to be permanently integrated within a dental suction/delivery system to generate a vacuum as part of the aspiration of water, blood, saliva, and tooth debris from the oral cavity of a patient and/or for connection to a dental cuspidor. It may or may not be designed to handle fluids (i.e., may be a wet or dry pump). It is not a complete system and does not include any patient contact devices.
