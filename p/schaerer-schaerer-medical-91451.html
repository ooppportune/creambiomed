Schaerer Medical 91451

by Schaerer Medical AG

Head rest standard axis 7300

What this type of device does: A device designed to be attached to an operating/examination/treatment table to support the head, and sometimes shoulders, of a recumbent patient, typically in preparation for and during many types of medical procedures or examinations conducted on such tables. It is available in various shapes and sizes and might include mechanisms to enable adjustments in relation to the table top. It does not include components intended for immobilization of the head (i.e., not a positioner). This is a reusable device after appropriate cleaning.
