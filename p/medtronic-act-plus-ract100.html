ACT Plus® RACT100

by Medtronic, INC.

INSTRUMENT RACT100 ACT PLUS 110V RECOND

What this type of device does: An electronic device that is used to measure, indicate, and/or record the length of time taken for plasma or a whole blood sample to form a clot following the initiation of a reaction, typically as part of in vitro coagulation studies.
