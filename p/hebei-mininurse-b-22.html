Mininurse B-22

by Hebei Pukang Medical Instruments CO., LTD

What this type of device does: A mechanical bed designed to be used as a patient bed for rest/sleep in a hospital ward/room with manual mechanisms to adjust the height and surface contour of the bed. It typically includes a framework with wheels, a mattress support platform that can articulate, a headboard and footboard, siderails, and mechanical manually-operated controls to operate the bed.
