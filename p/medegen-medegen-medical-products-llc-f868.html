Medegen Medical Products, LLC F868

by Medegen Medical Products, LLC

BAG: BIO 29X44 2.5ML RED, PR RL 200/CS

What this type of device does: An empty covered receptacle containing no additives or media intended to be used for the collection, and preservation and/or transport, of more than one type of clinical specimen (e.g., urine, faeces, sputum, mucous, tissue) derived from different body regions for in vitro diagnostic investigation. It does not contain patient-contact specimen sampling/extraction devices such as swabs/brushes. This is a single-use device.
