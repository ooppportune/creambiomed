ELMED 52224-26B

by Elmed Incorporated

5MM DIA., 24CM, DOUBLE CUP FUNDUS MAXI GRASPER, INSULATED SHAFT & HANDLE W/RF-POST, ROTATABLE-ONE PIECE W/FLUSHPORT

What this type of device does: An assembly of laparoscopes and their accessories used for the visual examination and treatment of the abdominal/retroperitoneal cavity and its organs (laparoscopy). The insertion is typically through an incision(s) made in the abdominal wall (routinely just below the umbilicus or in the near vicinity). This system is mostly used for gynaecological procedures involving the evaluation/treatment of abdominal or pelvic pain, ectopic pregnancy, ovarian cysts, appendicitis, or for female sterilization. This is a reusable device.
