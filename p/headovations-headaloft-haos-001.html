HEADALOFT HAOS-001

by Headovations LTD

What this type of device does: A rigid or non-rigid device, typically a strap, belt, bar, harness or vest designed to be attached to a wheelchair/chair/stander and/or its occupant to stabilize the occupant’s position for prevention of slips and/or falls from the appliance and/or to provide postural support. Some types may also be used to secure a wheelchair occupant in a wheelchair while travelling in a motor vehicle. This device may also include the extensions needed to attach this device and may be available in various sizes.
