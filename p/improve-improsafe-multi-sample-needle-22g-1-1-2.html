IMPROSAFE® Multi Sample Needle 22G × 1 1/2″

by Guangzhou Improve Medical Instruments CO., LTD.

What this type of device does: A sharp bevel-edged, hollow tubular metal instrument intended to be used as part of a blood collection set for drawing blood from a patient; it does not include wings for fixation/placement (i.e., not a venous butterfly/scalp vein needle). It is intended to be connected to blood collection tubing and/or a blood collection Luer connector for transfer into a blood specimen receptacle. It may include a pre-attached blood collection tube spike and/or a safety cover; however, it does not include blood collection tubing or a tube holder (i.e., not a set). This is a single-use device.
