Safety 1st IH601

by Dorel Juvenile Group, INC.

ESSENTIAL SICK-DAY SCREENING KIT

What this type of device does: A hand-held device intended to be used to displace the tongue to facilitate examination of the surrounding organs/tissues. It is typically a double-ended flat device with rounded working ends made of wood or plastic materials; it may be aromatic/flavoured to make it less intrusive. The device may be used in a healthcare facility, doctor's office, or in the home. This is a reusable device after appropriate cleaning.
