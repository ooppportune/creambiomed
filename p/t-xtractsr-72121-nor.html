XtractSR 72121 Nor

by T.s.g. Associates Llp

Xtract™SR Complete Kit - 72121 Nor

What this type of device does: A manual device in the form of a portable lightweight platform or sheet with handles on which a patient can be carried in an emergency from an area inaccessible to a conventional ambulance stretcher. It may be designed as a folding stretcher, rescue backet, spine board, or evacuation sheet, and may include patient straps to secure/stabilize the patient. This is a reusable device.
