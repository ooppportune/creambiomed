autoLog IQ™ ATLGIQR

by Medtronic, INC.

SYSTEM ATLGIQR AUTOLOG AUTOTRANS RECOND

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered device intended to be used in conjunction with a collection of appropriate containers and tubing (not included) to produce pressure for the collection of blood from a patient and the subsequent processing (filter/wash) of the blood components (e.g., red or white blood cells, platelets) as part of an autotransfusion procedure. It typically incorporates a peristaltic and aspiration (suction) pump. This is a reusable device.

autoLog IQ™ Autotransfusion System

The autoLog IQ™ autotransfusion system is an adaptable autotransfusion system that delivers a consistently high-quality blood product automatically.
