Horizon 6 Flex FA Centrifuge, Lavender 00-376-133-000

by Drucker Diagnostics

General purpose laboratory centrifuge for sample separation.

What this type of device does: An electrically-powered device intended to be used to separate the components of multiple types and various volumes of clinical suspensions using centrifugal force. It is a self-contained unit with an electric motor, a vertical shaft, and a horizontal rotor attached to the upper end. It is typically used to centrifuge various types of clinical specimens, either alone or after addition of reagents or other additives, for subsequent in vitro diagnostic analysis. It may additionally have refrigeration capabilities to perform temperature-sensitive procedures.
