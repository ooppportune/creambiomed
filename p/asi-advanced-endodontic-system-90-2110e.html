Advanced Endodontic System 90-2110E

by Asi Medical, INC.

Advanced Endodontic System

What this type of device does: A mains electricity (AC-powered) device designed to make available the necessary instruments/equipment and power to deliver dental treatment to a patient. It consists of a platform for the display of a selection of powered dental instruments (e.g., motor handpiece, scaler, suction, air/water spray), sometimes with a control panel for setting various operating parameters; it may be portable or mounted. The device is commonly referred to as a dental unit or dental operative unit and is intended to be operated by a healthcare professional in a medical facility.
