Specimen Collection Swab FANAB01

by Hlb CO.,ltd

What this type of device does: A piece of absorbent or non-absorbent material, with no additives, detached or on a stick, and intended to be used by a healthcare professional for the collection, and preservation and/or transport, of a saliva/oral, nasal, nasopharyngeal, and/or oropharyngeal specimen for culture, analysis, and/or other investigation. This is a single-use device.
