BD Vacutainer® C&S Boric Acid Kit 364959

KIT URIN TRANSTR 13X75 C&S KAGN CE HEMO

What this type of device does: A covered receptacle containing a pre-measured amount of vacuum and one or multiple preservatives [e.g., mercuric oxide, boric acid (H3BO3), benzoic acid, potassium phosphate, and/or sodium phosphate] intended to be used for the collection, and preservation and/or transport, of a urine specimen for culture, analysis, and/or other in vitro diagnostic investigation. This is a single-use device.
