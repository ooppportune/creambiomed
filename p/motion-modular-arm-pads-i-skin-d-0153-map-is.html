Modular Arm Pads (I-skin) D-0153-MAP-IS

by Motion Concepts INC

Modular Arm Pads (Highly durable skinned polyurethane resists cracking and peeling and provides extra comfort and protection for arms) Modular I-Skin Armpads are available in Desk length 10" or Full length 14". Mounting hardware included. Depth and width adjustable. Left and Right armrest configurations available. Optional wheelchair component for use with Motion Concepts power positioning systems.

What this type of device does: A small platform, typically cushioned, mounted on the lateral ends of a wheelchair to provide the user with a support on which to place the arms. The position of the platform (e.g., height) may be adjustable to suit the comfort and utility of the user.
