ULTRAFLO-PB 7392

by Innovative Medical Technologies, INC.

PUSH BUTTON SAFETY BLOOD COLLECTION AND INFUSION SET UTW 23GX3/4"X7" W LUER ADAPTER

What this type of device does: A collection of devices which includes a hypodermic needle (e.g., venous butterfly/scalp vein needle) intended to conduct fluids from an intravenous (IV) fluid container to a patient's venous system during administration; it may also be used for blood collection. In addition to the needle, the set may include tubing, connectors, chambers, clamps, gauze pads, alcohol pads and dressings; a bag/bottle may also be included. This is a single-use device.
