PolyDetector AEC HRP Red Ready-to-Use Substrate-Chromogen (3-Amino-9-ethylcarbazole) is a chromogen (color forming molecule) that develops into a red precipitate

What this type of device does: A substance or reactant that produces colour in response to a biochemical reaction and intended to be used in combination with other IVDs to test a clinical specimen, typically using an enzyme immunoassay or immunoblot method. Substrate examples include NBT, BCIP, TMB, 4CN, DAB.
